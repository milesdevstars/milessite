Logistics & notes

Book immediately

  • Marine Atlantic ferry (Port aux Basques → North Sydney) westbound — late Aug easier than eastbound but still book early. Book a cabin for the overnight crossing.
  • Canmore accommodation (peak July season)
  • Flights: YYC mid-July · YYT mid-August · YQY or YHZ → YYZ home Monday after ferry

Gear essentials

  • Bear spray — checked luggage only when flying. Buy in Calgary.
  • High-visibility vest — Trans-Canada truck traffic
  • Rain gear always accessible — NL fog rolls in fast
  • Lights front and rear — moose at dawn/dusk in NL

West coast alerts

  • Rogers Pass tunnel: call ahead, cyclists require escort
  • Kamloops heat: 35–40°C in July — ride before 9am
  • Hope wifi is poor — base at Chilliwack instead (30 km further)
  • Hwy 1A (vs Trans-Canada) for Calgary → Canmore — much nicer

Newfoundland alerts

  • Moose are a genuine hazard everywhere outside St. John's — dawn and dusk especially
  • Wreckhouse (near Port aux Basques on final day) — extreme crosswinds, check forecast before Sat ride
  • Interior NL: services 80+ km apart — always carry extra food/water
  • Deer Lake is a weekend stop only now — Corner Brook and Grand Falls are the work hubs
