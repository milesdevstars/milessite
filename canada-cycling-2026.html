Logistics & notes

Book / confirm

  • Via Rail Quebec City → Toronto with bike storage — book as early as possible, hooks are limited and not always guaranteed
  • Quebec City accommodation for 2 nights (Sep 10–12)
  • Trip 1 fully booked: Calgary Airbnb, Motel Bavaria, Golden cabin, Alpine Inn Revelstoke, Best Western Sicamous, Coldwater Hotel Merritt
  • One-off flight YVR → YYZ with boxed bike after Trip 1 — check oversized baggage policy

Gear essentials

  • Bear spray for Trip 1 (Rockies & interior BC) — checked luggage only when flying, buy in Calgary
  • High-visibility vest — both trips have stretches of highway traffic
  • Rain gear always accessible
  • Lights front and rear — wildlife and dawn/dusk riding on both trips

West coast alerts (Trip 1)

  • Rogers Pass tunnel: call ahead, cyclists require escort (if riding via Rogers Pass)
  • Kamloops heat: 33–38°C in August — ride before 9am
  • Hope wifi is poor — base at Chilliwack instead (30 km further)
  • Hwy 5A Kamloops→Merritt and Coldwater River trail avoid the busy Coquihalla

Toronto–Quebec alerts (Trip 2)

  • No flights, no bike box on this trip — rides start and end from home
  • Mon Sep 7 is Labour Day — cafes/co-work spaces in Montreal may have reduced hours
  • Route Verte (Quebec's long-distance cycling network) covers much of Montreal→Quebec City — mostly dedicated paths
  • Via Rail bike storage is the single biggest unknown of the whole 6 weeks — have a backup plan (local/regional trains, or a multi-day return ride if it comes to it)
